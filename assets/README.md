# Icon

The extension icon combines the avatar from [Destiny’s YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@destiny)
with a magnifying glass. The original channel artwork belongs to its respective owner.

The composite was made with the built-in imagegen tool. Prompt: preserve the channel’s
white geometric D and blue corners on a dark square; overlay a small white magnifying
glass on a dark badge in the bottom-right corner. Build sizes are generated from icon.png.
