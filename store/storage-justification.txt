TinySearch uses chrome.storage.local to save display and notification preferences, cache up to 200 recent public answers within seven days, and retain feed synchronization and release-check metadata across background-worker restarts. Notification enable time and delivered answer IDs prevent old or duplicate notifications. This supports searchable answer history, prevents duplicate cards, and avoids repeated network requests. These records are stored in the browser; search text, chat messages, chat drafts and general browsing URLs are not stored.
