TinySearch displays sourced research answers alongside Destiny.gg chat at https://www.destiny.gg/bigscreen.

When TinySearch's research service answers a question from Destiny's stream, a short TinySearch card appears in your chat view. Each card includes a complete summary, up to two source links, a timestamp, and a clipboard button.

Choose Full answer to read the detailed answer and its sources. The answer history lets you search previous questions, browse older and newer answers, filter for the current YouTube stream when it can be identified, and open the stream at the moment a question was heard.

The chat clipboard button copies the summary with an @Destiny prefix so you can paste it yourself. The detailed answer has a separate copy button for the full response and sources.

Use the extension popup to enable or disable TinySearch, control whether answers appear in chat, and choose compact cards.

Answers are generated by AI and can contain mistakes. Review the linked sources. Answer availability depends on the public stream and the TinySearch research service.

The extension uses the current DGG page and embedded stream identifier locally to place its interface and filter answers. Display preferences and recent public answers are stored in your browser. Public answer and release requests connect to TinySearch's hosted services; their host receives standard connection information such as IP address and request timing. See the privacy policy for details.

TinySearch is an independently developed extension for use with Destiny.gg.
